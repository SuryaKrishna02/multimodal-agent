What can I help with?

Type, talk, paste a picture or share your screen. I recall your memories, use tools, and run on this machine only.

Say "Hey Jarvis" with the wake word on, or press T to talk. Enter sends, Shift+Enter adds a line. Paste or drop a picture anywhere here. Type / for commands, such as /skill to teach me a new skill.

Tasks

Each one uses the whole agent: senses, memory, the model and tools.

Connect

Give the agent more tools. Every tool still passes the same permission checks.

Voice note to action items

Speak, stop, and get a transcript with a list of what to do. Both are saved to memory.

Look at my screen

The agent says what it sees and suggests a fix if something is wrong.

Ambient recall

Replay the recorded morning conversation as if it started at 09:00, or turn on always-on listening in . Then ask about it.

Daily brief

Writes today's brief from summaries, facts, action items and screen episodes. Tell it what to change, then reflect: the next version of its prompt appears in .

Speak up when a test fails

The agent looks at the screen every few seconds. When the screen changed, Gemma checks it for a failed test run, and a match is spoken, shown as a notification and written to the feed with the reason.

Spoken reminder

Say "Hey Jarvis, remind me to call Mike in two minutes". The chat answers through the reminders MCP server, and the daemon speaks the reminder when it is due.

      Voice
      
        
      The agent speaks with Kokoro, a small text-to-speech model that runs on this machine's CPU. A reply is
          spoken one sentence at a time, so the first sentence plays while the next one is written. The sound plays in
          this tab, because Google Meet shares only the audio of the tab that you present.
      

      
      Say something
      
        
      Turn on "Voice" at the top to play the sound in this tab. Without it, the agent speaks through this machine's speakers.
      
        
      
          
          
          
        
      
        
      
      

      
      Talk to it
      
        
      On the  page, turn on "Wake word" and say
          "Hey Jarvis", or press Talk (key T) and speak. Your request and the reply appear in the conversation, and the reply
          is spoken. Saying "Hey Jarvis" while the agent speaks stops it; without headphones the agent's own voice can drown
          out the wake word, so the Stop button (key S) is the sure way.

      How stopping works
      
        
      The Stop button (or the key S) writes a voice.stop event. Every process that is speaking
          checks for it before the next sentence, and this tab fades its sound out over a few milliseconds. The agent does not stop
          when the microphone hears just any voice, because its own voice from the speakers reaches the microphone too.
